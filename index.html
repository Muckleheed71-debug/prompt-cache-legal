Prompt Cache Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Draft — not yet published. Operator details, provider arrangements and younger-user protections still need confirmation.

The short version

Prompt Cache helps you save, organise and reuse prompts. You can use the local library without an account. Signing in links an account library across devices when cloud features are available. AI optimisation sends the text you choose to an AI provider. We do not automatically send messages in your AI chats.

This policy covers the Prompt Cache Android app, web app and browser extension. You can read it from About without signing in. The copy included in the app also works offline.

Who is responsible

Prompt Cache is provided by MuckleHeed, a sole trader business based in the United Kingdom. For support, privacy questions or an account-deletion request, email dev@muckleheed.dev.

We are responsible for deciding how personal information is used to run Prompt Cache. Contact us if you have a privacy question or want help with your information.

Your library and account

Your device or browser stores your prompts, categories, favourites, History, Trash, settings and unfinished work. Local-only content is not uploaded just because you open the app. Copies that you export or share are under your control.

When you sign in with Google, Google provides account details needed for sign-in, such as your email address, account identifier and available name or profile picture. We use Supabase to manage sign-in and the cloud account. We do not receive your Google password.

Signing in automatically links the account library when your plan allows sync. Saved prompts, categories, favourites, recent output versions and related sync records can then be stored on our backend and exchanged between your signed-in devices. Your original guest library is separate. Device-only drafts and settings are not all synced.

We also keep account, beta approval, trial, allowance and subscription records so we can provide access, count included AI requests and prevent repeated trials or misuse. You do not need to give account information to use the local library, but account-based features need sign-in.

AI optimisation and your optional profile

When you choose AI optimisation, the selected connection sends your prompt, the context needed for the request and the app's optimisation instructions to its AI provider. Included AI uses our server connection. A shared account API connection also passes through our backend. A device-only API connection sends requests using that connection.

If you choose to include your personal profile, all filled profile fields are included in that AI request. A profile can also be saved to your cloud account when sync is enabled. Leave out anything you do not want stored or sent. Avoid passwords, financial details, health details and other sensitive information.

Our included and shared AI request code asks the provider not to store responses using its API storage setting. That is not a promise of zero retention or a guarantee about training: the provider's terms, security logging and your account settings also apply. Other providers or gateways may handle requests differently.

Opening a prompt or reading this policy does not make an AI request. You review an AI result before replacing your original prompt. Copying, exporting or inserting text into another service puts that text under that service's rules once you send it.

Voice search and voice editing

Voice is optional and starts only when you press a microphone button and grant microphone access. You can review and correct the transcript before it is used for a search or sent to create an AI revision. Voice editing appears only for prompts and skills saved in your own library, not on Discover items.

For included voice, your recording is sent securely through Prompt Cache to OpenAI for transcription. If you use your own OpenAI connection, the recording is sent using that connection. Prompt Cache does not add recordings or transcripts to your prompt library, and its voice service does not retain the audio after the transcription request finishes. Service providers may keep limited security or abuse-prevention records under their own terms.

Recordings stop after 60 seconds. We keep account-level timing and request records needed to enforce monthly allowances and prevent misuse, without storing the recording or transcript in those allowance records. AI revisions may change meaning, so review names, numbers, placeholders, technical commands, tools and safety limits before saving.

API keys

An API key lets a provider charge requests to the account that owns it. Android stores a device-only key using its local security controls. A browser's device-only key is held for the current page session.

If you save an API connection to your account, the key is stored in the backend's encrypted secret store and used by the server for approved requests. Other devices can use that account connection without downloading the key. The app shows a mask rather than the saved key.

Keys are not included in your synced prompt library or normal library exports. Remove an account connection in AI settings if you no longer want it available across devices. You can also revoke the key with its provider.

Android accessibility and browser permissions

Android Accessibility is optional. When enabled, it helps Prompt Cache identify selected apps and supported browser pages, show the floating icon and read or insert text in an available chat input. If draft capture is enabled, the app can check that input in the background while the supported app is open. Draft capture is off by default. You can choose to enable it in widget settings.

Detected draft text is handled on your device for the capture feature. It is not automatically uploaded or saved as a prompt. Saving it to a synced library or asking for AI optimisation can send it as described above. You can turn off draft capture, choose fewer supported apps or disable Accessibility in Android settings.

The browser extension has access to its listed AI websites to show its launcher and insert text when you ask. It checks the relevant input field so it can avoid overwriting an existing draft. It does not need to upload the surrounding conversation to run those features. Clipboard access supports Copy; notifications support reminders. Website-installed Android builds can request installation permission for APK updates; Play builds use Google Play and do not request that permission.

Payments

Payments are currently being prepared and tested. When website subscriptions are available, Stripe will handle payment details. Prompt Cache does not receive or store your full card number or card security code.

We keep the customer and subscription identifiers, payment or subscription status, paid period and related records needed to unlock Pro and support your purchase. Stripe also handles personal information under its own privacy policy. Removing the app or signing out does not cancel a subscription.

Service providers, logs and sharing

We use Google for Google sign-in, Supabase for accounts, cloud storage and backend services, OpenAI for included AI, Stripe for payments when enabled, and hosting providers to deliver the app and website. Your chosen AI provider receives requests made through your own connection. Support email is also handled by the mail provider used for our contact address.

These services may process technical information such as your IP address, request times, errors and device or browser details to deliver requests, secure the service and investigate problems. We keep request and allowance records, but do not use the prompt-library database as a public directory.

The app has no advertising SDK or third-party advertising tracker. It uses device or browser storage to remember your library, preferences and sign-in. This is different from tracking you for advertising. Hosting and sign-in services may use their own necessary storage and security records.

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers as needed to run Prompt Cache, when you ask us to share it, or when law or a necessary security investigation requires it. Support access is limited to what is needed to deal with the issue.

Why we are allowed to use information

We use account, sync and access information to provide the service you request. The legal basis is providing our service under our agreement with you, where that basis applies.

We use limited security, abuse-prevention and support records for our legitimate interests in keeping the service reliable and helping users, balanced against your privacy rights. We keep records required by tax or accounting law because we have a legal obligation to do so.

If we rely on your consent for a particular use, we explain that separately. You can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful use. An Android permission or a button press is not blanket consent for unrelated uses of your information.

Where information is processed and how it is protected

The main Supabase database is currently hosted in Ireland. Other providers may process information in the UK, the United States or other countries. Their locations and international-transfer arrangements need to be confirmed before this draft is published.

We use encrypted network connections, account access checks and protected server secrets. Cloud sync is not end-to-end encrypted: authorised backend services can process synced content to provide the service. No system can promise complete security. Protect your device, Google account and API keys, and be careful about what you save or send.

Keeping and deleting information

Local copies remain until you delete them or remove the relevant app or browser data. Exports, screenshots, shared copies and other-device copies may remain separately. Signing out or uninstalling does not by itself delete your cloud account.

We keep your account and synced library while they are needed to provide your account service. You can delete prompt data in Settings. If sync is active, library deletions can sync to your other devices. Automated in-app account deletion is planned but has not yet been verified as available. At present, Settings → Account & sync → Request account deletion opens instructions for an email-based request to dev@muckleheed.dev. We verify ownership before processing requests; this action does not currently erase your account automatically. Public instructions are available at https://prompt-cache-ee096.web.app/delete-account.html. The intended release behaviour is a confirmed, self-service deletion flow that removes your account and associated cloud data, subject to the limited legal, security and payment-record retention described below. This notice will be updated when that feature is implemented and tested.

Some records may need to remain for security, disputed payments, legal requirements or accounting. We decide how long by the purpose of each record, applicable legal requirements and whether a dispute or investigation remains open. Backup copies and providers' own records can have separate deletion schedules. We will explain any relevant limits when dealing with a deletion request.

Your choices and rights

You can use the local library without signing in, export your prompts, remove saved prompt data, remove an account API connection and turn off optional permissions. An export of your prompt library is not necessarily a complete copy of all account records.

You can ask what personal information we hold, request a copy, correct it or ask for deletion. Depending on the circumstances, you can also ask us to limit its use or provide a portable copy. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before acting.

You have the right to object to uses based on our legitimate interests. Contact the privacy address and tell us which use concerns you.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, contact us so we can try to resolve it. You can also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or to the appropriate authority where you live.

Younger users

Prompt Cache does not currently impose an app-wide age limit. Google sign-in, payment services and individual AI providers can have their own age rules. Being able to open Prompt Cache does not override those rules.

If you are a child, ask a parent or trusted adult before sharing personal details, adding an API key or making a purchase. You or your parent can contact us for help with information stored in an account. The assessment of privacy protections for younger users is still outstanding for this draft.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date when this policy changes. If a change materially affects how your information is used, we will bring it to your attention through the app or another suitable channel. A policy update does not give us automatic permission for a new use that needs your consent.
